About Me

I'm a third-year informatics student at the University of Oslo. What I enjoy most is taking something technical from a lecture and finding out where it actually lives in the real world. Like learning algorithms, then seeing they are what makes the WWW work.

The highlight so far: building SydVest, an Android weather app, with a team of six. We worked in weekly sprints with pull requests and code review. Which is as close to a real engineering team as a course gets. Between that, coursework, and personal projects, I've written code in Java, Kotlin, Python and C.

My latest project is Purrjury, a daily logic puzzle about lying cats. My algorithms classes helped a ton here: making sure every puzzle has exactly one answer, and that you can always reach it with logic alone, is a search problem through and through.
